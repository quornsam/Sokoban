# BOXXY Privacy Information

Effective: 20 August 2026

BOXXY is delivered through Cloudflare. Accounts are optional; the game remains playable without one.

## Information stored in your browser

BOXXY stores game progress, manually saved gameplay positions, settings, Level Maker saves, custom solutions, Level Pack drafts and Daily Puzzle drafts using browser local storage. Clearing browser site data may erase these records.

If you do not sign in, normal game progress remains on that device only.

## Optional BOXXY accounts

If you create an account, BOXXY stores your chosen username, email address, a one-way password hash, account dates, your sign-up and most recent IP address, most recent browser/device user-agent, accumulated active time while signed in, and the game progress/settings needed for cloud saving.

Account information and cloud progress are stored in Cloudflare D1. They are used to provide the account, synchronise progress between devices, administer BOXXY and prevent abuse. Passwords are not stored in readable form.

A secure, strictly necessary session cookie is used while you are signed in. Account email addresses are not used for advertising. They may be used where reasonably necessary for account, security, abuse or support matters.

You can delete your account from the account screen. This removes the account and cloud copy of its progress; progress already stored in your browser remains on that device.

## Analytics

BOXXY uses PostHog, hosted in the European Union, in cookieless mode to measure visits and a limited set of gameplay events. BOXXY does not send account usernames or email addresses to PostHog and session recording is disabled.

PostHog and Cloudflare receive ordinary technical information needed to deliver and measure web requests, which may include IP address, browser/device information, page path, referrer and request time.

## Shared-puzzle links

A shared custom-puzzle link contains an encoded puzzle title and starting layout in the URL fragment. The link is unlisted, not encrypted. Anyone who receives it can decode and play the puzzle.

## Contact

If you use the Contact Us form, the name, email address and message you provide are used to deal with that communication.

Privacy questions may be sent to `archedroof+boxxy@gmail.com`.
