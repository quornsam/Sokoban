# Commercial release checklist

Before selling or monetising BOXXY, obtain professional legal advice and resolve the following third-party matters:

- confirm commercial permission for the Microban levels, or replace them with original levels;
- do not commercially distribute the CC BY-NC-ND packs without separate permission from their creators;
- obtain a compatible licence or written permission for the Rust/WebAssembly solver backend, or replace/remove it;
- confirm the ownership or commercial-use terms of every music, sound and image asset;
- consider a UK trade mark search and application for the BOXXY name/logo;
- consider registered-design protection for distinctive interface or character designs;
- decide whether copyright should remain with Sam Cornwell personally or be assigned in writing to a company.
