BOXXY v135 — Guided Solve Cancel patch

Replace these three files in the root of your GitHub Sokoban repository:

- index.html
- styles.css
- boxxy.js

The patch adds a visible STOP GUIDED SOLVE button while guided playback is running.
The Escape key also stops playback. The board remains at its current position so the
player may continue manually or restart. Any completion after a partially used guided
solve remains correctly marked as guided.

No other files are changed.
