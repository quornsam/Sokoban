A Daily Sokoban-style puzzle

Daily Boxxy gives players one shared box-pushing puzzle for the day. Solve it at your own pace, then compare approaches through the optional signed-in leaderboards.

PLAY BOXXYHOW TO PLAY

One puzzle, different ways to solve it

The Daily uses the same core BOXXY rules: move through the board and push every box onto a target. The leaderboard then lets signed-in players compare two different kinds of result without changing the puzzle itself.

Fastest Times

For players who enjoy speed, eligible completed runs can be compared by completion time.

Fewest Moves

For players who prefer efficiency, the leaderboard can also compare the lowest move count achieved on the puzzle.

No account required to play

You can play the Daily without making an account. Signing in is optional and is used when you want your result associated with a public username.

Come back tomorrow

The Daily format gives BOXXY a small, shared challenge alongside its larger collection of ordinary puzzle levels and packs.
