BOXXY v120 — MAZE-SUBSET DEADLOCK TEST REPORT
==============================================

Solver-core version: 5.5.0
Search engine: forward Feature Space Search (FESS)

Defect corrected
----------------
The previous permissive local-window proof treated boxes already standing on goals as solved and removed them from that local pattern. That is safe for that individual optimistic proof, but it created a serious blind spot because a goal box can complete a frozen, diagonal or otherwise interacting deadlock. The locked position supplied from Small Chessboards 12 therefore passed the complete deadlock gate, entered FESS and was incorrectly retained as a best position.

v120 adds a separate generic maze-specific connected-subset proof which retains goal boxes in the tested group. Nearby groups of two to four boxes are tested with every other box removed. This gives the selected boxes more freedom than the real position. A branch is rejected only when every legal continuation in that easier subproblem reaches an already proved structural deadlock. Unfinished analysis returns unknown rather than dead.
