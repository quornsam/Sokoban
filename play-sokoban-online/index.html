BOXXY is a free Sokoban-style box pushing puzzle game that runs directly in your browser. No download is required, and you can start playing without creating an account.

A modern box-pushing puzzle game

Push every box onto a target without trapping it against a wall or in a dead corner. BOXXY keeps the simple logic that makes Sokoban satisfying, then adds handcrafted original puzzles, Daily challenges, optional leaderboards and controls designed for both desktop and phone.

Handcrafted puzzles

Play original BOXXY levels alongside established puzzle collections. The emphasis is on deliberate puzzle design rather than generated filler.

Daily Boxxy

A fresh Daily puzzle gives returning players a shared challenge, with fastest-time and fewest-moves leaderboards for signed-in players.

Browser first

Play on desktop or mobile without installing a conventional game client. Progress is kept on the device, with an optional account for cloud sync.

Play your way

Keyboard, touch and accessibility-minded play options sit alongside undo, restart and puzzle navigation tools.
